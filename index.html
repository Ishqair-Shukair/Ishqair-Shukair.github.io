About

Systematic, calm, and focused on the fix

I'm a Computer Science graduate from DePaul University with nearly four years of hands-on technical support as a Geek Squad lead at Best Buy. I resolve about 15 support cases a day across Windows and macOS systems, mobile devices, and home networks, taking each issue from first diagnosis to verified fix.

My approach is systematic. I isolate the root cause before touching anything, whether it's failing storage, a memory fault, malware, or a misconfigured network. I document the resolution and explain it so non-technical users understand what happened and how to avoid it. I also mentor technicians through escalated repairs and manage our repair queue so work stays on schedule.

I'm most interested in the infrastructure side of IT: keeping systems reliable, protecting data, and automating repetitive work so people can focus on their real jobs. My CS training covers operating systems, databases, algorithms, and software development, and I've applied it by building a full-stack personal finance app with Django, PostgreSQL, and Docker.
